The original author describes this as a stereo field recording loop of wind in
forest / trees, recorded using a Tascam DR-05. The MP3 above is the publicly
provided high-quality preview, not the original 24-bit WAV.

The bundled MP3 is unchanged. During playback the app applies an 0.8-second
overlap at the loop boundary, a common level adjustment, high-pass filtering at
180 Hz and low-pass filtering at 6.5 kHz. It mixes the recording quietly with
original synthesized bird phrases and occasional short notes. Continuous
synthetic insect and pitched chord layers have been removed.
